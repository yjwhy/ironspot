# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: 18 May 2026
**Last Updated**: 18 May 2026

IronSpot ("the Service") respects your privacy and complies with the Republic of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and other applicable laws. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of personal data we collect, how we use them, and the rights you have over your data.

This policy is a translation of the Korean original. Where there is any conflict between language versions, the Korean version prevails.

## 1. Purposes of Processing

We process personal data to:

- Create and maintain user accounts
- Show nearby gyms and equipment based on your location
- Moderate and verify user-uploaded gym photos
- Process reports of inappropriate content
- Analyse usage statistics and improve service quality
- Track errors and respond to outages

## 2. Categories of Data We Process

| Category                | Data items                                                                                 | Collected when                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Required (signup)       | Email address                                                                              | At account creation                     |
| Optional (app use)      | Precise location (GPS coordinates)                                                         | When you grant the location permission  |
| Optional (photo upload) | Photos of gym equipment (uploads containing identifiable faces are automatically rejected) | When you upload a photo                 |
| Automatic               | Device identifiers, OS version, app version, IP address, access logs, error logs           | On app launch / when an error is raised |

## 3. Retention Periods

- Account data: retained until **you delete your account**, then deleted immediately.
- Uploaded photos: retained until you delete them or until they are removed through moderation.
- Automatic logs (access / error): retained for **90 days** and then destroyed.

Where retention is required by law (e.g. commercial-transaction records), we will retain the relevant data for the legally mandated period. The Service does not currently process payments, so no such statutory retention applies.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except:

- with your prior consent;
- where required by law or in response to a lawful request from a competent authority.

## 6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

- request access to your personal data;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- request deletion of your data;
- request that we stop processing your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 11. We will respond promptly.

## 7. Security Measures

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
- Database access control and row-level security (RLS).
- One-way hashing of authentication credentials.
- Automatic rejection of uploaded photos in which a face can be identified.

## 8. Children Under 14

The Service is not directed to and does not permit account registration by children under 14. Accounts identified as belonging to children under 14 will be deleted immediately.

## 9. Automatically Collected Data and Tracking

The Service does not use advertising identifiers or tracking cookies. You may reset operating-system-level advertising identifiers from your device settings.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy due to changes in law, policy, or the Service. Updates will be posted on this page with at least **7 days' notice** before they take effect (or **30 days' notice** for changes that materially affect users' rights).
